Headliner repair & replacement in Houston

Roof fabric sagging onto your head? We pull the headliner board, strip the old fabric and redo it properly, so it stays up.

Why headliners sag

The foam gives out.

There's a thin foam layer between the fabric and the board. Heat and age break it down until it crumbles.

Glue can't fix it.

Spray glue or pins on the old fabric don't hold for long, because the foam it's stuck to is what failed.

The fix is new material.

The board comes out, gets stripped down, and gets new fabric bonded to it.

The process

How a headliner job usually goes

  1. 1

    Board comes out.

    Trim, lights and handles come off and the headliner board slides out of the car.

  2. 2

    Stripped and recovered.

    The old fabric and crumbling foam come off, and new fabric is bonded to the clean board.

  3. 3

    Back in, tight.

    The board goes back in and everything gets reinstalled.
