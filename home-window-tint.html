Why tint your windows

Cooler rooms.

West-facing rooms that cook every afternoon stay a lot more comfortable, and the AC doesn't have to fight as hard.

Less UV and glare.

Film cuts the UV that fades floors, furniture and rugs, and the glare on TVs and screens.

Daytime privacy.

Neighbors and people walking by see less of what's inside, without blinds closed all day.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does home tint cost?

It's priced by the number and size of the windows. Text us a rough count, or a couple of photos, and we'll text a quote back.

Will it make my rooms dark?

Not unless you want it to. There are light films that cut heat without looking tinted. We'll show you options before anything goes on the glass.

Can people see in at night?

Most privacy films work in daylight. At night, with lights on inside, people outside can see in, so you'll still want blinds after dark.

My old film is bubbling. Can you fix it?

Ask us about taking the old film off and putting new film on. Send a photo with your quote request.

Do you do businesses?

Yes. Storefronts and offices too. Tell us the address and roughly how many windows.
