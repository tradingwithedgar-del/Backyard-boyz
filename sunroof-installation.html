Sunroof or moonroof?

Sunroof.

A panel in the roof that tilts or slides open to let in light and air.

Moonroof.

A tinted glass panel that lets light in even when it's closed, and usually tilts or slides open too.

Panoramic.

A large glass roof that stretches over the front and back seats, often with a section that opens.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does a sunroof cost?

It depends on the vehicle and the type you want. A pop-up and a panoramic roof are very different jobs. Text us your year, make and model and the type you're after, and we'll text you a quote.

Which type is right for my car?

Tell us your vehicle and what you want from it, more light, more air or the full open-roof feel, and we'll tell you which types fit and what each costs.

What's the difference between a sunroof and a moonroof?

A sunroof is a panel that opens to let in light and air. A moonroof is a tinted glass panel that lets light in even when it's closed. People often use the names for the same thing, and we install both.

Can the glass be tinted?

Ask us about tinting the sunroof or moonroof glass when you request your quote.

How long does it take?

It depends on the vehicle and the type. We'll give you a time with your quote so you can plan around it.
