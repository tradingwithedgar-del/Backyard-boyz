FAQ

Common questions

How much does a mobile mechanic visit cost?

There's a $75 travel and diagnostic fee to come out and find the problem. If you book the repair, the $75 comes off the bill. The repair itself is quoted separately.

What does roadside assistance cost?

A roadside call is a $75 call-out. That covers jump starts, flat tire changes, lockouts and fuel delivery.

Are you open at night and on weekends?

Yes. We're open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Where do you come out to?

We're mobile across Houston, Katy, Jersey Village, Tomball, The Woodlands, Spring, Pasadena, Humble, Kingwood, Friendswood, Missouri City, Bellaire, Stafford and Cloverleaf, and our shop is in Cypress.

What should I tell you when I call?

Your year, make and model, what the car is doing (or not doing), and where it's parked. A photo of any warning light on the dash helps.
