Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Backyard Boyz Window Tinting ("we," "us") is a family-owned window tinting and auto glass business serving the Houston, TX and Cypress area. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it. We keep it simple, because we keep our business simple.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us. When you use the quote form, that means:

  • Your name
  • Your cell phone number
  • The service you selected and any vehicle or property details you type in

We do not run advertising trackers or analytics on this site, and we do not set our own cookies. The spam-protection tool on our form (hCaptcha) may collect limited device and usage information to tell real visitors apart from bots.

Why we collect it

We use the information you submit for one reason: to get back to you with a quote and to schedule your service. That's it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information, and we don't share it for marketing. To run the website and the quote form, we rely on a few trusted services that process your submission on our behalf:

  • Web3Forms — delivers your quote request from the website to our email inbox. See web3forms.com.
  • hCaptcha — protects the form from spam and abuse. See the hCaptcha Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website itself.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests only as long as we need them to serve you and keep a record of the job. If you'd like us to delete your information, just ask (see below) and we'll take care of it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what we have on file, correct it, or delete it. Reach out using the contact info below and we'll handle it promptly.
