FAQ

Questions we get every week

How much does tint cost?

It depends on the vehicle, how many windows and which film. A coupe and a crew-cab truck aren't the same job. Send the quote form or text us your year, make and model and we'll text you a real number.

What's legal in Texas?

The front side windows have to let in at least 25% of light. The back side windows and rear glass can go darker if you have side mirrors on both sides. The windshield can only have a strip across the top. We'll tell you what's legal for your vehicle before we start.

Can you come to me?

Yes. We're fully mobile around Houston, or you can bring it to our shop in Cypress. Just tell us where when you request your quote.

How long does it take?

It depends on the job. We'll give you a time when we text your quote so you can plan your day.

When can I roll my windows down?

Give the film a few days to cure before you roll the windows down. We'll tell you exactly how long for your film when we finish.

Will it bubble or turn purple?

Bubbling and purple fade usually come from cheap film or a rushed install. Ask us which films we carry and what they cover when we quote.

Do you do houses and businesses?

Yes. Home and commercial window tint for heat, UV and privacy. Send the form with "Home or commercial" and a rough count of windows.
