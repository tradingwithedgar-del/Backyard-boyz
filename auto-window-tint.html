FAQ

Common questions

How much does car tint cost?

It depends on the vehicle, how many windows and which film. A coupe and a crew-cab truck aren't the same job. Send the quote form or text us your year, make and model and we'll text you a real number.

What's the darkest I can go in Texas?

The front side windows have to let in at least 25% of light. The back side windows and rear glass can go darker if you have side mirrors on both sides. We'll tell you what's legal for your vehicle before we start. This is a summary, not legal advice.

What do the percentages mean?

The number is how much light gets through. 70% is barely there, 35% is the classic look, 20% is dark and 5% is limo. Try them on the G-wagon on our homepage.

When can I roll my windows down?

Give the film a few days to cure first. We'll tell you exactly how long for your film when we finish.

Will it bubble or turn purple?

Bubbling and purple fade usually come from cheap film or a rushed install. Ask us which films we carry and what they cover when we quote.

Can you come to me?

Yes. We're fully mobile around Houston, or you can bring it to our shop in Cypress.
